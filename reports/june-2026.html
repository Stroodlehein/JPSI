Executive Summary

Japan physical silver outperformed COMEX through June's decline.

JPMI-AG fell 14.8% during June, while COMEX silver converted into JPY/g fell 21.4%. The result was a widening physical premium, with the month ending at +19.19%.

Japan's physical silver market remained resilient in June even as international silver pricing weakened sharply. JPMI-AG began the month at ¥422.73/g and ended at ¥360.32/g, a monthly decline of 14.8%. Over the same period, COMEX silver converted into yen per gram declined from ¥384.81/g to ¥302.30/g, a sharper fall of 21.4%.

The divergence between domestic physical pricing and international futures pricing caused the JPMI physical premium to remain persistently elevated. The premium averaged 12.52% during the month, ranged from a low of +4.43% on 11 June to a high of +20.16% on 5 June, and finished the month at +19.19%.

Refinery and dealer pricing declined alongside global markets, but retail street pricing, represented by the Mercari MSPI-B component, remained comparatively firm. Mercari observations ranged from ¥443.00/g to ¥533.00/g, reinforcing the gap between institutional-style buyback pricing and peer-to-peer physical silver transactions in Japan.

Premium Analysis

The physical premium remained positive all month and widened into the close.

The JPMI premium averaged +12.52% in June. The lowest observed premium was +4.43% on 11 June, while the highest was +20.16% on 5 June. The final observation on 29 June recorded +19.19%, indicating that the month ended in an elevated premium environment.

The premium did not rise because Japanese physical prices increased in nominal terms. Instead, it widened because COMEX prices weakened more aggressively than Japan's composite physical index. This distinction is important: the market signal is not simply "higher physical prices," but rather relative physical resilience.

Refinery & Dealer Analysis

Refinery and dealer pricing moved lower, but remained orderly.

Refinery Spot (RS) averaged ¥371.57/g in June, with observations ranging from ¥314.38/g to ¥418.44/g. Dealer Bid (DB) averaged ¥358.95/g, ranging from ¥309.00/g to ¥402.00/g.

The average RS-DB spread was ¥12.62/g. This relationship remained relatively stable despite volatility in global pricing, suggesting that domestic buyback markets adjusted in an orderly fashion rather than showing major internal dislocation.

Mercari Physical Market

Retail street prices stayed elevated despite broader silver weakness.

Mercari MSPI-B averaged ¥482.56/g during June. The highest observed street-market level was ¥533.00/g on 5 June, while the lowest was ¥443.00/g on 29 June.

Even at the end of the month, Mercari remained materially above both RS and DB. On 29 June, Mercari was recorded at ¥443.00/g, compared with RS at ¥322.96/g, DB at ¥315.00/g, and COMEX at ¥302.30/g.

Outlook

July will test whether elevated premiums persist.

The key question for July is whether the elevated premium environment observed into late June proves temporary or becomes a more persistent feature of Japan's physical silver market.

If COMEX stabilises while Japanese physical prices remain firm, JPMI premiums may continue to indicate meaningful divergence between paper and physical markets. Conversely, if Japanese refinery and dealer bid prices catch down more aggressively, the premium could compress.

JPMI will monitor:

  • Whether the physical premium remains above the June average of +12.52%.
  • Whether Mercari MSPI-B remains structurally above RS and DB.
  • Whether dealer bid pricing continues tracking refinery buybacks in an orderly way.
  • Whether Mercari qualifying transaction counts suggest improving or weakening liquidity.
  • Whether COMEX USD/oz movements translate proportionally into Japan physical pricing.
JPMI's July focus: premium persistence, retail liquidity, and the degree of physical market resilience relative to global futures pricing.

Methodology & Notes

Data sources and construction methodology.

The Japan Physical Metals Index™ (JPMI) is an independent research initiative tracking Japan's physical precious metals market through publicly observable price data.

JPMI-AG combines three major physical silver market layers: Refinery Spot (RS), Dealer Bid (DB), and Mercari Street Physical Index (MSPI-B). The current composite weighting framework is RS 55%, DB 30%, and MSPI-B 15% when all components are available.

Refinery Spot includes major Japanese refinery silver buyback prices. Dealer Bid includes observable dealer and pawn bid pricing for Sv1000 silver. MSPI-B reflects qualifying one-ounce bullion coin transactions observed on Mercari.

COMEX silver is converted into Japanese yen per gram using prevailing USD/JPY exchange rates where available. Premium calculations compare JPMI-AG against the COMEX yen-per-gram equivalent.

Historical observations are recorded as daily snapshots around 19:00 JST on trading days. Some historical observations were reconstructed from JPMI Git commit history and cleaned for obvious outliers where appropriate.

This report is not investment advice. Data may contain delays, missing values, market anomalies, or errors. JPMI is designed as a market intelligence and research tool, not as a trading recommendation service.
