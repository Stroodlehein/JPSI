1. Executive Summary

Japan's physical silver market remained structurally firm during June even as international paper-market pricing weakened sharply. JPMI-AG declined from ¥422.73/g on 1 June to ¥360.32/g on 29 June, a fall of approximately 14.8%. COMEX equivalent pricing declined more severely, from ¥384.81/g to ¥302.30/g, a decline of approximately 21.4%.

The result was a persistent physical premium environment. The average JPMI premium versus COMEX was +12.26%, with the month ending at +19.19%. The highest premium was +20.16% on 5 June, while the lowest was +4.43% on 11 June.

Mercari street market prices remained the most resilient component of the dataset, averaging ¥483.28/g and ranging between ¥443.00/g and ¥533.00/g. This suggests that retail physical silver pricing remained elevated even as global benchmark prices declined.

2. JPMI-AG Monthly Overview

JPMI-AG averaged ¥384.86/g across 21 June observations. The index reached its monthly high of ¥424.39/g on 2 June and its monthly low of ¥336.45/g on 25 June.

The monthly path showed two distinct phases: early-month strength above ¥410/g, followed by a sharp repricing after 5 June as both refinery and dealer quotes moved lower. Despite that move, JPMI-AG held materially above COMEX equivalent pricing through most of the month.

3. Premium vs COMEX

The physical premium averaged +12.26% during June. Premiums were below 10% on 5 observation days, above 15% on 3 days, and above 20% on 1 day.

The widening premium was primarily driven by COMEX equivalent pricing falling faster than the domestic physical composite. This is the core divergence JPMI is designed to capture: Japan's physical market does not always move one-for-one with international futures pricing.

The average RS-DB spread was ¥11.42/g. This indicates that the domestic buyback structure remained orderly even during the wider market repricing.

5. Mercari Street Market

Mercari MSPI-B averaged ¥483.28/g, significantly above JPMI-AG and COMEX equivalent pricing. The highest Mercari observation was ¥533.00/g on 5 June, while the lowest was ¥443.00/g on 29 June.

The average Mercari premium over COMEX equivalent pricing was approximately +41.36%. Even as COMEX weakened, retail physical pricing remained elevated, reinforcing the importance of tracking Japan's street-level bullion market separately from refinery and dealer buyback markets.

6. Key Observations

  • Japan physical outperformed COMEX: JPMI-AG fell about 14.8%, while COMEX equivalent pricing fell about 21.4%.
  • Premiums remained elevated: the average physical premium was +12.26%, with month-end premium at +19.19%.
  • Retail pricing stayed firm: Mercari averaged ¥483.28/g, roughly ¥98.42/g above JPMI-AG.
  • Domestic buyback markets remained orderly: RS and DB moved lower together, with an average spread of ¥11.42/g.
  • COMEX USD declined sharply: recorded COMEX USD/oz fell from $75.05/oz to a late-month low of $57.54/oz.

7. Data Notes & Methodology

JPMI-AG combines Refinery Spot (RS), Dealer Bid (DB), and Mercari Street Physical Index (MSPI-B) to estimate Japan's physical silver market level. Premium calculations compare JPMI-AG with COMEX silver converted into Japanese yen per gram using the corresponding foreign exchange data where available.

8. Outlook for July

July analysis should focus on whether Japan's elevated physical premium remains persistent or compresses as global prices stabilise. If COMEX recovers while domestic retail pricing remains resilient, the key metric will be whether JPMI-AG continues to maintain a double-digit premium.

JPMI will continue monitoring daily 19:00 JST snapshots, the RS-DB relationship, Mercari transaction quality, and the spread between Japan's physical market and international futures pricing.
