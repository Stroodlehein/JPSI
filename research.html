JPMI focus

JPMI Research is observational. It does not attempt to predict future silver prices. The goal is to track how local physical markets behave relative to global benchmarks.

The recurring question: when global pricing and local physical pricing diverge, what does that say about access, availability, currency pressure, and market structure?
